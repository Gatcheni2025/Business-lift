Data Deletion Instructions

You can request deletion of personal information associated with your Teyza account and data received through connected platforms.

Effective: 28 September 2026

Request deletion from Teyza

To request deletion, contact Teyza through the support channel available on teyza.co.za and clearly state that you are requesting account and personal-data deletion.

Please include

  • The email address or phone number used for your Teyza account.
  • Your business or seller name, if applicable.
  • A short statement confirming that you want your Teyza account and associated personal information deleted.

We may ask you to verify ownership of the account before completing the request. We will not ask for your password.

Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp data

If you connected a Meta account to Teyza, you may first disconnect the relevant Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp Business connection from Teyza's Sales Channels page. You can then request deletion of the stored connection data using the process above.

Following a valid request, Teyza will delete or de-identify personal information and connected-platform credentials that are no longer required, subject to information we must retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes.

What may remain

Some information may remain in backups for a limited period or be retained where required by law. Transaction records may also need to be retained where South African tax, accounting or consumer-protection requirements apply. Where retention is required, the information will no longer be used for unrelated purposes.

Removing Teyza from Meta

You may also remove Teyza's access from the relevant Facebook/Meta account settings. Removing access stops future authorised access from that connection, but it does not automatically delete information already lawfully stored by Teyza. Use the deletion process above if you also want stored Teyza data deleted.

Deletion request reference: When you submit a request, keep the confirmation or support reference you receive. Teyza may use it to update you on the request.
