ONBOARDING COMMISSION

R99 once per eligible shop

Commission becomes eligible after Teyza approves your work and the seller, and the new shop makes its first full R99 subscription payment. Later monthly subscriptions go to Teyza.

This is the onboarding commission component, not a guaranteed salary. Employment or contractor status, any applicable wage guarantee, travel expenses and engagement terms are agreed before work starts.

Every visit has a clear checklist

01 / GET APPROVED

Your own agent account

Apply with your working area and contact details. Wait for Teyza approval before starting visits.

02 / RECORD THE VISIT

Shop photo, selfie and location

Get the owner’s permission. Capture the shop, yourself inside it and a current device location. These are private review materials, not public shop pictures.

03 / OWNER CONFIRMATION

The owner stays in control

Share the confirmation link. The owner signs in with their own shop account and completes their mandate privately. Submit your visit for review and track the outcome.

Your photos and location support a manual work review by Teyza.

Working across South Africa

Applications can identify a working area in any of the nine provinces. Teyza approves agents and operating areas as capacity becomes available; applying does not guarantee an assignment.

Never collect a shop owner’s password, OTP or bank account details. Owners complete those steps themselves.

Ready to apply?

You will need your own account and a smartphone with camera, internet and location access. There is no agent application fee.
