HOW IT WORKS

Your journey from application to reward

Register online and sign in to Teyza with the same account. The app gives approved agents their own dashboard, map and visit history. Registered businesses are distinguished from public map prospects.

01 / APPLY ONLINE

Your approved agent account

Submit your contact details, working area, ID or passport and CV. Wait for approval; no application fee.

02 / SIGN IN & INVITE

Visit and invite businesses

Use the app's Field Agent dashboard. Visit with permission, record the business location and take the required private photos. Invite owners through WhatsApp.

03 / VERIFY & EARN

Track qualifying rewards

The owner registers on their own account and confirms the visit. Once the new seller and your work are verified, Admin can approve one R90 reward. Track the status in your dashboard.

Your photos and location are private work evidence. A registration or invitation alone does not establish earnings or payment.

Work across South Africa, one area at a time

Applications can identify a working area in any of the nine provinces. Teyza approves agents and operating areas as capacity becomes available; applying does not guarantee an assignment.

Never collect a business owner’s password, OTP or bank account details. Owners complete those steps themselves.

Ready to connect local businesses?

You will need your own account and a smartphone with camera, internet and location access. There is no agent application fee.
