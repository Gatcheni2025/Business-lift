# Teyza national business acquisition — schema 2 candidate

Development branch only. Production-tested base: da06e0ff64ee068d94812af65e6e0a34b3eceb15 (draft PR #236). PR #237 is stacked on that branch. Do not merge or publish without separate operator approval.

## Contact queues and evidence
- All nine provinces; retained prospects include businesses with no phone/email, which go to the field-visit queue, not the default new-contact queue. WhatsApp-only is explicit and is not voice-call permission. Source and channel-specific owner consent are required.
- Staff-confirmed contact and provider acceptance are separate evidence. A provider accepted message is not proven delivery, and a WhatsApp link opening is not a sent message. Reconcile historical contact, sent email and provider-accepted invitation events, including events beyond the 25 displayed in record history. Unclear earlier progress or a queued invitation blocks another initial invitation and routes to review. No historical records are rewritten or deleted.
- Downloads mean explicit owner confirmation, not install telemetry. Registration requires a genuine unique linked seller; approval and membership are read from the existing seller/verification and billing records. No conversion event approves a seller or charges a customer.

## Government reference and identities
- The read-only locality adapter uses the published DPME ArcGIS P_Dm_M_Lm_Sp_W_Bound FeatureServer (sub-place layer 0; local municipality layer 2, 2018 vintage), official municipality codes and metropolitan names. Source and vintage are retained with the selected locality. Official municipal boundaries can change. This is NOT a verified complete 2026 national township register. Unavailable, ambiguous or unlisted places can be saved provisionally for review. The server re-resolves official references and never trusts a client-supplied label as verified.
- Existing Durban and Johannesburg document IDs, place links, seller links and events are preserved. Exact normalized province/name/address identity keys and transactional legacy-pilot matching prevent identified duplicates. The original city/area/address/name ID is checked as well. Cross-province collisions and uncertain matches require manual review, not automatic merging. Legacy scan stops at 5,000 matches to avoid unsafe partial deduplication. No production backfill is performed by this candidate.
- Google discovery is still ephemeral; only independently confirmed, permitted business details are saved. Discovery coverage depends on the external source. No scraping or bulk messages.

## National reporting contract
- ops_acquisition_command_page returns schema 2, fixed asOf, validated filters, count deltas, a cursor and completion flag. Every page reads the same Firestore read-only snapshot; all joined evidence uses that read time. First/list pages scan 30 records; counts-only continuation pages scan 60. Exact official codes or category use indexed equality where possible; remaining filters are evaluated server-side, including unmigrated legacy rows. No counts are limited to the visible list.
- Admin aggregates every cursor page, shows unavailable totals until the scan completes, rejects inconsistent/duplicate cursor pages and clears stale totals after a mutation. Incomplete reports may resume while their snapshot is valid (55 minutes). Queue counts are independent and overlap, rather than a mutually exclusive pipeline. Filtering a queue changes matching rows, not the geographic/category aggregate denominator. Follow-up due today uses SAST.
- Large ledgers beyond 10,000 relevant historical events or campaigns per prospect fail closed. Verify read-time and query/index behavior, latency, cost, rate limits and scale with staging data before national rollout; a materialized reporting index may be needed for very large volumes.

## Commercial reconciliation
- Paid seller means existing live Paystack membership entitlement with valid paid-until, not complimentary access, test payments, mandate-processing or a manually recorded charge.
- Verified active paid ads require an approved/live eligible shop with a valid location, an active campaign in its display window, valid campaign price and coordinates, and a matching verified paid live ZAR transaction with the same owner, campaign, amount, reference and gateway transaction ID. Unpaid, expired, paused, sandbox, mismatched, reversed, refunded or missing evidence cannot qualify. Interest and unresolved ad reconciliation remain separate.
- Unique paying businesses count the union of paid sellers and verified active paid advertisers, not the sum. No billing or campaign state changes are made by reporting.

## Performance candidate (27 November 2026)
- Batch 1 uses page-scoped Firestore transaction.getAll for deduplicated suppression, linked seller and commercial dependency reads; grouped ownerUid IN campaign queries; bounded concurrent legacy ledger reads. The exact report preserves readTime, and action checks are unchanged. UI throttles only near its report request limit.
- Batch 2 implements 27 audited additional whole-municipality predecessor codes (31 total), retains provenance, excludes four divided municipalities from official assignment, and merges province/pilot-city index streams without duplicates. No prospect history migration or spatial certification is implied. The public probe checks one reference per predecessor and nine province samples.
- Batch 3 adds immutable report generations, precomputed scope aggregates, redacted queue rows indexed by a combined hashed scope/queue array key, and a read-only summary reader with a five-minute expiry and SAST day boundary. An explicit exact refresh, arbitrary text search, unsupported filter intersections, and unavailable or expired summaries use the exact report. The summary opens live details only, never authorizes a send/payment/approval. Existing security rules deny direct client access to the new collections. No new composite index is declared; the new province equality, ownerUid IN and lookupKeys array-contains/document-ID queries require cloud confirmation. No rules or deployment configuration changes. The operator/job collector and atomic publisher are implemented, but scheduled refresh, retention/cleanup, and staged publication are separate release gates. Builds are bounded to 20,000 prospects / 80,000 scopes and must finish within the five-minute freshness window to publish; partition/refresh strategy is required at larger scale. The three-second goal is unverified until actual staged cached reads and browser load are measured. Existing exact-read path and current production remain unchanged until separately approved release.
