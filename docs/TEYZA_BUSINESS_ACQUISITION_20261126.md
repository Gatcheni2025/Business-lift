# Teyza Business Acquisition Command Centre — first release

Pilot: Durban and Johannesburg. Suggested work areas are displayed as planning guidance, never as eligibility restrictions on owners. Businesses outside the pilot may use normal seller registration. Field-agent records and earned rewards remain separate.

Admin-only access uses existing verified recent-admin authentication and rate limits. Store prospects in private server-only teyzaBusinessProspects, events subcollections, contact suppression and unique seller links. Existing rules must not grant direct client access to these collections; verify deployed rules before release. No collection of owner identity documents or credentials; no automatic communication, scraping, imports or outbound email/WhatsApp/phone initiation. Retain contacts only from authorised sources; record explicit channel-specific owner permission and actual activity before logging outreach. Opt-out suppresses known phone/email across duplicate records. Suppression cannot be removed from this workspace. Duplicate detection uses normalised name/city/area/address; variant spellings may need human review. Follow-up is an internal due date, not an automatic send or reminder. Activity events are append-only, timestamped and attributed to the authenticated reviewer. Creation and activity are saved transactionally after explicit Save; no persistent browser draft or keystroke autosave. Existing seller link requires exact name and UID plus owner confirmation, unique seller linkage, and live read of approval and public listing status; it does not create, approve or change seller accounts or payments. Counts cover loaded pages and do not claim causal attribution or newly acquired sales. The latest 25 events are displayed per record. No cost or conversion metrics until reconciled with actual spend and full cohorts.

Deployment: review backend and browser CI, scoped publisher tests, rules, and actual signed-in Admin smoke test. Restore and compare existing runtime configuration, health, and exact secret bindings before isolated teyzaMobile deploy; post-check payment/access health and adminBusinessAcquisition:true. Then node tools/app-first-release/publish-business-acquisition-only.cjs deploy. Publisher changes the nine Admin HTML aliases plus app-admin JS, operations JS and new acquisition module, preserving other site assets and settings. Rollback Hosting using the same publisher rollback. No mobile, pay, recruitment campaign, Zoho worker or field-agent email automation changes. Before enabling direct provider sending, review legal basis and opt-out handling, provider permissions and approved templates, and implement a dedicated safe queue. No live messages are sent by this change.

Next steps: actual business-interest callback form, owner-controlled invitations, permitted lead capture, staff-specific acquisition roles and assignments, paginated complete activity history, data retention/correction workflow, partner referrals, and approved Zoho/WhatsApp provider delivery integrations. Existing applicant communications and recruitment closure must be handled separately.

## Email and owner help desk

The acquisition register filters businesses ready for contact, linked registered accounts, invitation/welcome logs, help requests and opt-outs. Load the existing verified mapped Teyza inventory in pages, search by city/township/category/name, or open the national coverage map for ephemeral Google local results. This is not a census of all businesses; no Google data export. Copy personalised invitation or welcome templates and manually send from the official Zoho mailbox. Record actual SAST sent time and reference only after staff confirms matching Zoho Sent; no claimed provider acknowledgement/delivery. One record per email type, invitation requires recorded email consent and no opt-out. Service welcome requires genuine linked seller and matching owner email; no marketing content or automatic send. The owner can request help even after opting out of marketing; actual replies and resolutions are recorded. Read-only summaries of linked seller support cases and a button opening the existing two-way seller support inbox are included. Current email credentials have send scope, not inbox read scope: provider-confirmed sends and synchronised email replies need a separately reviewed Zoho integration, explicit per-message send approval, suitable read scopes and privacy controls. No automatic outbound calls or messages. Staff can open Zoho and use existing seller-app/WhatsApp support as available.
