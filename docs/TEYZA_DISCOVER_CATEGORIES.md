# Teyza 1.0.46 (57): Discover categories

The second bottom navigation destination is now **Discover**. Its category list
replaces the duplicate Nearby shops page. Home retains nearby shop and business
discovery, and the centre Shop destination retains published seller products.

Discover uses the bundled product catalogue as a general reference guide. Its
photographic category rows open product lists. Search matches product names,
categories, units and catalogue keywords. Back returns to categories before
leaving Discover. No shop stock, price, delivery promise or Teyza verification
is inferred from a category or Google Maps listing.

**Ask a shop** opens a product-specific choice of nearby spaza shops, using the
same classification, distance and duplicate-removal rules as Home. A verified
Teyza merchant with live chat opens an unsent product enquiry in Teyza chat;
other listings can open an unsent WhatsApp enquiry to their public number.
Google phone details are requested only after the user chooses that shop.
The draft asks the shop to confirm availability, brand, pack size and price.
Missing messaging details offer the shop profile for other contact options.

This screen does not claim that Google supplies an inventory feed. Shoppers
still review and send the enquiry themselves. The guide is available offline
after installation; contacting shops needs the usual connection and messaging
application. Navigation, category/search/back handling, large text, loading
recovery and the Google-listing enquiry handoff have widget coverage.
