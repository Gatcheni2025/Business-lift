# Teyza R3.7 — realistic everyday essentials

The eight category photographs replace the SVG grocery illustrations in the Everyday Essentials shelf. They are bundled in `apps/teyza_shopper/assets/images/essentials/` and loaded with `Image.asset`, so they do not depend on a product-image host or the Google Places backend.

These are generic category photographs. The shelf does not claim a specific merchant SKU, price or stock availability. Existing merchant-uploaded catalogue photos retain their separate product flow.

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. Original transparency is preserved; deployment copies use WebP encoding at 640-pixel thumbnail resolution to keep the Android package compact. App version: 1.0.5 (15).
