Is registration really free?+

Yes. Registration and verification are free. Approved free business profiles may appear in local discovery. Eligible product-selling functionality requires an active paid membership; view current seller plans and terms.

What do I need for verification?+

Complete the required ID or passport, owner selfie, shop-front photo, location and phone verification in your own account. Verification and listing activation are separate. Our team can help you navigate the process.

Can spaza shops and wholesalers connect?+

Eligible approved spaza buyers can connect with participating suppliers and review trade offers or request stock. Wholesalers set their own pricing, quantities and delivery terms. Offers and services depend on eligibility and availability.

Can customers order or contact me?+

Relevant seller features include eligible catalogues, customer communication and supported order requests, depending on approval, membership and enabled services. Delivery and collection depend on the shop. Teyza does not guarantee orders, funding, discounts or free delivery.

I cannot find the app in my store. What now?+

Availability depends on country, device and store account. Use the official links above or ask Teyza support for help. Avoid unofficial app downloads.
