UNDERSTAND TEYZA

Your local shop,
easier to find.

Teyza connects shoppers with spaza shops and other local retailers. Shops manage their own products, prices, stock and fulfilment. Teyza helps people discover and contact them.

Get the Teyza app ↗

From finding a shop to collecting your order

01 / DISCOVER

Choose your area.

Use your location or select an area to find nearby shops. Public map-directory listings can appear alongside participating Teyza shops.

02 / CHECK THE SHOP

See what the owner offers.

Look at published products, the shop photo, opening hours and collection or delivery options. A directory listing alone does not mean a shop takes Teyza orders.

03 / CONNECT

Message, order or get directions.

Where enabled, send the shop a message or order request. Check the total and any delivery fee before sending. The shop confirms stock and fulfilment.

Who handles delivery and payment?

Each participating shop decides whether to offer delivery, its fee and area, and whether it accepts cards at the shop. The order flow shows the available options. Payment is arranged directly with the shop.

Teyza does not promise delivery in every area, instant fulfilment or that every map listing has connected stock. Check with the owner before travelling or paying.

Two ways to use Teyza

Shopper: discover, browse and contact local shops.

Shop owner: register in the app, complete verification, wait for approval and manage your selling workspace.
