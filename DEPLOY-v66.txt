TEYZA SELL PRODUCT + SERVICE FIX v66

ROOT CAUSE
==========
dashboard.html already pointed the Sell button to sell.html,
but js/dashboard-data.js was overwriting it after the seller readiness
check and changing the link back to:

  products.html#new-product

That is why clicking Sell opened the Product Publisher directly.

FIX
===
dashboard-data.js now keeps the primary seller action as:

  sell.html

The flow is now:

Dashboard
  -> Sell
  -> What do you want to sell?
       -> Product
       -> Service

UPLOAD HOTFIX
=============

/dashboard.html

/sell.html
