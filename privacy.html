Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Teyza processes personal information when people use our website, seller tools, marketplace features and connected services.

Effective: 28 September 2026

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to buyers, sellers, business representatives, visitors and other users of Teyza services. Where South African law applies, Teyza processes personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

2. Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, phone number and authentication identifiers.
  • Seller and business information, including business name, addresses, verification information, banking or settlement details, and product information.
  • Order, delivery, customer-service and marketplace activity.
  • Device, browser, security, log and usage information needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Information received from services you choose to connect, including Google, Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp Business, subject to the permissions you approve.

3. Why we use personal information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify sellers, operate stores and product listings, process orders, enable customer and seller communication, connect authorised sales channels, prevent fraud and abuse, provide support, maintain records and comply with legal obligations.

4. Connected platforms

If you connect a third-party platform, Teyza may receive and store identifiers, access tokens and business-asset information needed to provide that connection. For Meta services, this may include Facebook Page identifiers, Instagram professional-account identifiers, WhatsApp Business Account identifiers and phone-number identifiers. Teyza uses this information only to provide the connection and features you request.

5. Sharing of information

We may share information with service providers that support hosting, authentication, messaging, payments, delivery, analytics, security and connected-platform functionality. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, Teyza or the public. We do not sell personal information.

6. Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Connected sales channels can be disconnected from Teyza. See our Data Deletion Instructions for account and Meta-platform deletion requests.

8. Children

Teyza is intended for people who are legally able to enter into the relevant transactions and agreements. We do not knowingly design the seller platform for children.

9. International processing

Some technology and platform providers may process information outside South Africa. Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to ensure that cross-border processing is subject to appropriate safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Teyza develops or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

11. Contact

Privacy requests: Please contact Teyza through the support channel available on teyza.co.za. For deletion requests, follow the process on our Data Deletion page.
