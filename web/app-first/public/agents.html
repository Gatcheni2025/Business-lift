HOW IT WORKS

Your journey from application to reward

Register online and sign in to Teyza with the same account. The app gives approved agents their own dashboard, map and visit history. Registered businesses are distinguished from public map prospects.

01 / APPLY ONLINE

Your approved agent account

Submit your contact details, working area, ID or passport and CV. Wait for approval; no application fee.

02 / SIGN IN & INVITE

Visit and invite businesses

Use the app's Field Agent dashboard. Visit with permission, record the business location and take the required private photos. Invite owners through WhatsApp.

03 / VERIFY & EARN

Track qualifying rewards

The owner registers on their own account and confirms the visit. Once the new seller and your work are verified, Admin can approve one R90 reward. Track the status in your dashboard.

Your photos and location are private work evidence. A registration or invitation alone does not establish earnings or payment. Only an eligible verified new business may earn a reward.

BEFORE YOU APPLY

Questions about Teyza Work

What is a complete signup?

A new eligible seller registers using their own account after your visit, confirms your private invitation, and has their registration and your visit approved by Teyza. Existing sellers and duplicate referrals do not qualify.

When will I be paid?

Approved rewards are recorded as awaiting payment. Payment timing and engagement and expense terms must be agreed before work starts. There is no guaranteed income or assignment.

Can I invite other agents?

Yes. Share this public application page with interested people. They apply independently and are subject to Admin approval. No payment is offered for recruiting agents.

Share application via WhatsApp

What do I need?

A smartphone with camera, internet and location access, your own sign-in account, ID or passport and a CV. Use the secure application page, never WhatsApp, to submit identity documents.

Read the work privacy notice

This is a performance-based opportunity. Work availability and earnings are uncertain and depend on approval, eligible business opportunities, completed verification and agreed terms.
