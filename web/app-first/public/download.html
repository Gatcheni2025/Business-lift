One app. Local shopping and wholesale sourcing.

Shoppers: discover local shops and browse everyday essentials.

Spaza owners: register for free, verify your shop and choose Basic to browse eligible wholesale stock offers. Wholesalers: register, verify and publish your own bulk stock catalogue for approved spaza buyers. Other retailers can register separately.
