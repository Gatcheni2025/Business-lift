GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? We've got you.

Clear answers before you get started.

Visit support
Is Teyza free to join?+

Shoppers can use Teyza to discover nearby shops. Business registration is free. Approved shop profiles may appear in local discovery; publishing products and other paid seller tools require eligible membership and verified activation.

Can every shop accept orders through Teyza?+

No. Google-linked directory results are for discovery. Only participating Teyza shops with enabled products and services can receive eligible orders. Look for the shop's available actions in the app.

Does Teyza deliver everywhere?+

Each seller decides whether to offer delivery, its area and fee. Collection is available where enabled. Always review the shop's current information.

Where can I download Teyza?+

Use the official Google Play and Apple App Store links on our download page. Public availability depends on store review and country rollout. Approved Android testers can use the testing link.
