WHAT IS TEYZA?

A connection between local shops and their neighbourhoods.

Teyza is a South African app for discovering spaza shops and local retailers. Participating shops manage their own products, prices, stock and service options. Shoppers can browse published ranges, message enabled shops, request orders and get directions.

For shoppers

Find nearby shops and understand what each one offers. Collection, delivery and payment options depend on the shop.

FOR SHOP OWNERS

Your shop journey starts in the app.

Website seller sign-up has moved to the Teyza app. Open the app, choose Shop Owner, and follow the guided registration.

  1. Create or sign in to your Teyza account.
  2. Add your shop category, business details and location.
  3. Complete the mobile number and identity verification requested in the app.
  4. Manage your shop profile and products from the app. Your shop goes live after the required setup and approval.

Registration does not guarantee approval or immediate publication. Shop plans and any applicable charges are shown in the app.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few helpful answers.

How do I download Teyza?

Android early access is available through the Google Play testing link for approved testers. The public iPhone release is coming soon. Visit Get the app for the current availability.

Can I register my shop on this website?

Seller registration now takes place in the Teyza app. Choose Shop Owner and follow the registration and verification steps. This website introduces Teyza and provides app access and support information.

Does Teyza work across South Africa?

Teyza is built for South African shoppers and local retailers. Discovery results, participating shops, products and services depend on the area you select. Coverage is still growing.

Does every shop offer delivery?

No. Collection, delivery, stock and pricing depend on the individual participating shop. Confirm the available options with the shop in the app.

Will my shop appear immediately?

A new seller must complete the required setup and verification. Publication is subject to approval and the shop’s listing status.

How can I get help?

Email support@teyza.co.za. For a login or OTP issue, include your app version and the error message. Never send passwords or SMS codes.
