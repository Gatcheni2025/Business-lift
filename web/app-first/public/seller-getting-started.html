Your shop is ready for its next step.

Welcome to Teyza. Sign in to your seller workspace and build your local presence at your own pace.

1

Sign in to your shop

Open the Teyza app and sign in with the same account you used for registration. Choose the seller workspace.

2

Check your business details

Confirm your shop name, full address and map pin. Add a shop-front photo and set your opening and service options.

3

Choose when to add products

Registration is free. When available, product access is R49 for the first eligible month and R99 monthly thereafter, unless an administrator grants an exception.

4

Add your stock

Once product access is active, choose products and enter your own prices and stock. Check whether they are visible to shoppers.

5

Respond to your customers

Use your seller dashboard to check messages and eligible orders. Turn on notifications and keep your shop details current.

Payment availability
Supported checkout options and product access depend on your app version and store. No payment is taken simply by registering or reading this guide. Contact support if payment is not yet available.
