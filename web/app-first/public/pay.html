How we protect your payment

  • Correct business account: the payment attaches to your signed-in Firebase UID, never a typed email or URL parameter.
  • Verified seller: Teyza checks seller registration, selfie approval, phone OTP and shop setup on the server.
  • Secure checkout: your card details are entered only on Paystack's hosted payment page.
  • Confirmed payments: Firebase verifies Paystack server-side and activates only the original seller UID. Returning from Paystack alone does not prove payment.
  • Return to the app: reopen the Teyza app after payment. It checks your verified selling access automatically when you return and while your dashboard is open.
  • Cancel: manage an active subscription using the secured Paystack management link after signing in.
