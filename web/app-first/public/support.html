QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

A few common issues you can solve before contacting us.

Download information
The app isn't appearing in the store.+

Google Play and Apple App Store availability may vary by country and review status. Try the official download links and verify your device's store region.

I can't sign in.+

Confirm you're using the correct login method. Check connectivity and use the in-app password recovery option if available. Contact support if verification continues to fail.

My approved shop isn't visible nearby.+

Confirm that your shop is approved, its location is saved correctly and the current listing meets Teyza's publication requirements. Report the missing listing with your business name and area.

I need to delete my account.+

Start with the in-app account deletion option. You can also read our account deletion information and contact support if the app option is unavailable.
