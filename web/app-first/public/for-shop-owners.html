Get your shop ready, step by step

01 / YOUR ACCOUNT

Choose Shop Owner.

Sign in with your own account and register your shop category, name, contact details and full selected address.

02 / VERIFICATION

Complete the owner checklist.

Submit the required ID or passport, private verification selfie, shop-front photo and verified mobile details. Prepare products while your application is reviewed.

03 / APPROVAL

Activate your local listing.

Registration alone does not make a shop public. Paid listings require completed verification, admin approval and current verified R99 Basic payment. Free shops can continue preparing products privately.

One workspace for your shop

Products and shop photo

Choose from product templates, enter your own prices and available stock, and keep your shop image current.

Orders and customer messages

Respond to shoppers and manage collection or delivery requests. Enable phone order alerts on your device and check the in-app inbox.

Your service options

Set delivery availability and fees, card acceptance at the shop, trading hours and messaging. Finance is a separate enquiry and review, not a guaranteed benefit.

What R99 Basic can help your business access

Business finance and stock loans

Submit a business funding enquiry for assessment by an eligible provider. Approval and lending terms are not guaranteed.

Wholesale stock discounts

Explore supplier deals and lower wholesale prices when participating partners offer them.

Qualifying free stock delivery

Delivery fees may be waived on eligible wholesale stock orders in participating areas, subject to supplier and order conditions.

Sell at competitive retail prices

Better buying prices can help you price competitively and improve your potential margins. Savings and sales vary.

Discounts, finance and delivery offers depend on partners, eligibility and availability. R99 Basic includes local listing priority after verified activation; it does not guarantee orders, loans, free delivery or profit.
