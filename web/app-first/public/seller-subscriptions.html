BEFORE YOU JOIN

Clarity at every step.

The important details behind registration, approval and paid selling.

Can I register without paying?+

Yes. Registration and verification are free. Eligible approved shop profiles can appear in local discovery; a current paid membership is required for eligible product-selling and order tools.

Is the monthly membership R99?+

Yes. New eligible sellers may pay R10 for the remainder of their first calendar month, followed by R99 recurring on the first of the next month and each subsequent month until cancelled. Returning subscribers may pay R99 initially. Checkout remains disabled until the secure Paystack rollout is activated.

Do paid plans guarantee wholesale deals or sales?+

No. Wholesale offers, delivery discounts, finance and customer orders depend on partner availability, eligibility and shop activity.

Can I pay through the store app today?+

Purchasing options depend on app-store policy, the current app build and rollout. If checkout is unavailable, no in-app charge is taken. Do not pay to unofficial accounts.
