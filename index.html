GOOD TO KNOW

Start local. Build with confidence.

Fast shopping should not depend on unrealistic delivery promises. Local coverage, stock and delivery times will be confirmed during the pilot.

Does this website sell products to shoppers?

No. Shop owners use teyza.co.za to manage their businesses. Customers will use the Teyza Android/iOS app.

Will my shop appear in the app as soon as I register?

Only after merchant verification and marketplace integration have been configured and tested.

What happens if my shop cannot deliver?

You can offer customer collection where enabled. Local delivery depends on each shop's supported operations.

Can I offer services?

Yes. Services begin with enquiries and quotations, with appointments and payment integrations added after testing.
