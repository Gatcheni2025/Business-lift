Questions

Good to know before you start

Set up at your own pace. Your dashboard will guide you through the next step.

How do I set up my business?

Create an account, complete your business details and verification, then choose delivery and payment options.

Can I add products once?

Yes. Add a product with its image, description and price in your workspace, then choose the channels you connect for publishing.

Is Teyza for South African sellers?

Yes. The workspace supports rand pricing, local business details and delivery settings for sellers in South Africa.
