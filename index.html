QUESTIONS

Good to know before you start

Your workspace guides you through the next step, and your progress is saved as you go.

What do I need to create a seller account?

Start with your business details and seller profile. Teyza then guides you through phone verification, location, supporting documents, identity checks, delivery, payment details and audience setup.

Do I have to connect every selling channel?

No. Teyza Store is included automatically. External selling channels are optional and can be connected when the seller has the required account and provider access.

Can I add a product once and choose where it sells?

Yes. Create the product in your Teyza catalog, then choose from the connected selling channels available to that seller account.

Can I offer services as well as products?

Yes. A seller can create bookable services with pricing, location, availability and booking rules. Customers can discover those services in Teyza Store and submit a booking or payment flow supported by the service setup.

What happens after a customer buys?

The sale can be followed from the seller workspace so orders, customers and delivery work stay connected.

Is Teyza designed for South African businesses?

Yes. The experience is built around South African seller details, rand pricing and local business workflows.
