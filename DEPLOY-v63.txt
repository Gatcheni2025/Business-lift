TEYZA PAYSTACK SOUTH AFRICA BANK VALIDATION v63

WHY THE ERROR HAPPENED
======================
v62 used GET /bank/resolve.
That endpoint is for Nigeria/Ghana account resolution.

South Africa must use Paystack:
  POST /bank/validate

This fixes:
  Please supply one of the following valid currencies: NGN, USD, GHS, KES

NEW FLOW
========
Bank
Account number
Account holder name
Account ownership: Personal / Business
Identification type
ID / Passport / Business registration number
Confirm details
Verify & connect payout account

BACKEND
=======
Banks:
  GET /bank?currency=ZAR&enabled_for_verification=true

Validation:
  POST /bank/validate
  country_code = ZA

After successful validation:
- creates or updates seller Paystack subaccount
- stores only masked account details + subaccount code
- does NOT store full account number
- does NOT store ID/passport/business registration number
