TEYZA FOR SHOP OWNERS

Your shop. Connected through the app.

Register as a Shop Owner in Teyza, complete verification and manage your shop profile and products.

Register free. Upgrade when you want to be discovered.

Create your business profile at no charge. After registration, choose Free or R99 Basic in the Teyza app. Selecting a plan does not take payment or make your shop public.

Free · R0

  • Register your shop and submit verification documents.
  • Prepare your product catalogue and stock prices.
  • Manage your private shop profile and upgrade later.

Free shops are not shown in nearby shopper search results.

Basic · R99/month

After admin approval and verified payment, eligible shops can appear in local shopper searches with featured positioning.

  • Be more visible to shoppers in your trading area and receive enquiries or order requests.
  • Business finance or stock-loan enquiries, assessed by participating lenders; approval is not guaranteed.
  • Access to participating wholesalers' discounted stock offers, where available.
  • Free stock delivery on qualifying wholesale orders, subject to partner coverage and order conditions.
  • Wholesale savings can help you set competitive retail prices and protect margins; results vary.

Finance, discounts and delivery offers are subject to eligibility, partner availability and individual terms. The R99 plan does not guarantee sales, credit, savings or free delivery on every order. Payment and plan activation must be verified separately.
