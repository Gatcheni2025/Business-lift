BEFORE YOU JOIN

Clarity at every step.

The important details behind registration, approval and paid selling.

Can I register without paying?+

Yes. Registration and verification are free. Approved shop profiles may appear in local discovery. Product selling needs membership or administrator-granted access, subject to checkout availability.

Is R49 the monthly subscription price?+

R49 is the introductory first-month offer for eligible new sellers. The recurring price is R99 per month after the introductory period, subject to the enabled payment channel.

Do paid plans guarantee wholesale deals or sales?+

No. Wholesale offers, delivery discounts, finance and customer orders depend on partner availability, eligibility and shop activity.

Can I pay through the store app today?+

Purchasing options depend on app-store policy, the current app build and rollout. If checkout is unavailable, no in-app charge is taken. Do not pay to unofficial accounts.
